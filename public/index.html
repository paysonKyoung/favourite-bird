Lammergeier

Lammergeiers are remarkable mountain-dwelling birds of prey. They are famous for their unusual diet and behavior: they feed primarily on bones and will drop large bones from great heights onto rocks to break them into pieces they can eat.
Image credits: Matěj Baťha CC BY-SA 3.0

3 fun facts

  • Lammergier are large vultures, with wingspans reaching nearly 3 meters. This is impressively large, even for other vulture species.
  • Up to around 90% of their diet can consist of bones, requiring their stomach acid to be highly potent.
  • They carry large bones high into the air and drop them onto rocky slopes, sometimes repeatedly, until the bones break into smaller pieces.

    • Highlights

  • Unlike most vultures, lammergiers eat mostly bones and bone marrow rather than soft tissue.
  • Their distinctive orange-red coloration comes partly from bathing and rubbing themselves in iron-rich soil, which can often be a method of claiming dominance.
  • Their broad wings and long, wedge-shaped tails allow them to soar efficiently over mountainous terrain.
