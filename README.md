    <h1>Lammergeier</h1>
    Lammergeiers are remarkable mountain-dwelling birds of prey. They are famous for their unusual diet and behaviour: they feed primarily on bones and will drop large bones from great heights onto rocks to break them into pieces they can eat.

  <h1>3 fun facts</h1>
  <li>Lammergier are large vultures, with wingspans reaching nearly 3 meters. This is impressively large, even for other vulture species.</li>
  <li>Up to around 90% of their diet can consist of bones, requiring their stomach acid to be highly potent.</li>
  <li>They carry large bones high into the air and drop them onto rocky slopes, sometimes repeatedly, until the bones break into smaller pieces.</li>
  <br>
  <h1>Highlights</h1>
  <li>Unlike most vultures, lammergeiers eat mostly bones and bone marrow rather than soft tissue.</li>
  <li>Their distinctive orange-red coloration comes partly from bathing and rubbing themselves in iron-rich soil, which can often be a method of claiming dominance.</li>
  <li>Their broad wings and long, wedge-shaped tails allow them to soar efficiently over mountainous terrain.</li>
